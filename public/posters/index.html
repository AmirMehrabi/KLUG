Edit event.js to update all twelve live previews. Set the meeting name. Enter the Solar Hijri date as day month year and set the time, address, and portrait path. The portrait path is relative to poster.html.

To make new PNGs, open a live preview in Chrome DevTools device mode, set the viewport to 1600 × 900 for desktop 1080 × 1920 for Story, 480 × 380 for mobile, or 1535 × 530 for slider, and capture a screenshot at device pixel ratio 1.
